3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information        to operate, improve, and     secure the Platform.

    ●    Operate Hunts

    ●    Respond     to customer support requests

    ●    Send   transaction   confirmations

    ●    Send   payout notifications
    ●    Improve Platform functionality
    ●    Develop    new features
    ●    Measure Platform performance
    ●    Comply     with  legal obligations
    ●    Enforce our Terms of Service

AI-Assisted Verification

To  help review proof submissions and         detect potential fraud, Huntz may        use artificial intelligence systems
provided   by  trusted  third-party  service providers, including      Anthropic's Claude models and         similar
technologies.

AI systems may      assist in:

    ●    Reviewing     proof submissions
    ●    Detecting    suspicious activity
    ●    Identifying   duplicate or manipulated       submissions
    ●    Flagging    possible fraud
    ●    Supporting    moderation     decisions

Automated     systems do   not by   themselves determine Hunt outcomes.

Whenever an     AI system flags a submission        that could  affect a user's stake, payout, or eligibility, the
submission    is reviewed   by  a person   before a final decision    is made.

Users may    dispute verification    decisions in   accordance with     our Terms of Service.

AI Training

We do   not use your proof submissions to        train publicly  available third-party    AI models.

Where available, we configure AI service providers to            process submissions without using         them to
improve or train    public foundation    models.

Marketing

With  your consent, we may       send  newsletters, announcements, promotional emails, and             product updates.

You  may   unsubscribe at any     time using   the unsubscribe link     in our emails or through     your account
settings.

Certain  transactional communications—such            as payment confirmations, dispute notices, proof reminders,
and  security  notifications—cannot be disabled        while you    have an   active account or active Hunt.
