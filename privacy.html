Huntz Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 30, 2026

Welcome to Huntz ("Huntz," "we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect your personal information when you use the Huntz mobile application, our website at huntz.ai, and any related services (collectively, the "Platform").

By creating an account or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the Platform.

Contents

2Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use the Platform.

Information You Provide

Account Information

When creating an account, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Username
  • Email address
  • Password
  • Date of birth

We use this information to create and secure your account, verify your eligibility, and personalize your experience.

Identity and Eligibility Information

To determine whether you are eligible to participate in certain Hunts, we may collect:

  • State or country of residence
  • Information necessary to verify your identity where required by law or our fraud-prevention procedures.

Payment Information

If you participate in Hunts involving entry fees or payouts, we collect information necessary to process payments, including:

  • Bank account information
  • ACH authorization
  • Transaction history
  • Payout information

Payment processing is performed by third-party payment providers. Huntz does not store your complete payment card information.

Hunt Activity

When participating in Hunts, we collect information relating to your participation, including:

  • Hunts joined
  • Entry fees
  • Progress submissions
  • Photos
  • Videos
  • Written reflections
  • Completion history
  • Verification outcomes

This information allows us to operate Hunts, determine outcomes, prevent fraud, and improve the Platform.

Communications

We collect information when you communicate with us, including:

  • Customer support emails
  • In-app support requests
  • Dispute submissions
  • Survey responses
  • Feedback

Host Applications

If you apply to become a Hunt Host, we may collect:

  • Application responses
  • Connected social media accounts
  • Audience information
  • Other information you choose to provide during the application process

Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Platform, we automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Device model
  • App version
  • IP address
  • Device identifiers
  • Crash reports
  • Browser type
  • Session information
  • Usage analytics
  • Features accessed
  • Time spent using the Platform

We may also derive your approximate location from your IP address to determine jurisdictional eligibility and improve security. We do not collect precise GPS location unless you separately choose to enable a future feature requiring location.

Information From Third Parties

We may receive information from third-party providers, including:

Payment Providers

Our payment processors may provide:

  • Transaction confirmations
  • Payment status
  • Fraud prevention signals
  • Verification information

Connected Social Accounts

If you choose to connect a supported social media account (such as TikTok) to verify Hunt participation, we receive only the information you authorize through that platform.

We do not receive your password.

We do not post content on your behalf without your permission.

You may disconnect linked accounts at any time through your account settings or through the social platform.

Service Providers

We receive information from vendors that help us provide our services, including analytics providers, cloud hosting providers, customer support platforms, and security providers.

Information We Do Not Collect

Unless specifically stated otherwise, Huntz does not collect:

  • Biometric identifiers such as facial geometry templates, fingerprints, or voiceprints
  • Precise GPS location
  • Contact lists from your device
  • Health information from Apple Health, Google Health Connect, or similar device health APIs

Although users may voluntarily upload photos or videos as proof for Hunt verification, Huntz does not use those images to create or store biometric identifiers.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18.

3How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to operate, improve, and secure the Platform.

Specifically, we use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Verify your identity and eligibility
  • Operate Hunts
  • Record proof submissions
  • Verify Hunt completion
  • Calculate payouts
  • Administer dispute processes
  • Process payments
  • Detect fraud
  • Prevent abuse
  • Prevent multiple or fake accounts
  • Protect Platform security
  • Respond to customer support requests
  • Communicate important account updates
  • Send transaction confirmations
  • Send proof reminders
  • Send payout notifications
  • Improve Platform functionality
  • Develop new features
  • Measure Platform performance
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Enforce our Terms of Service

AI-Assisted Verification

To help review proof submissions and detect potential fraud, Huntz may use artificial intelligence systems provided by trusted third-party service providers, including Anthropic's Claude models and similar technologies.

AI systems may assist in:

  • Reviewing proof submissions
  • Detecting suspicious activity
  • Identifying duplicate or manipulated submissions
  • Flagging possible fraud
  • Supporting moderation decisions

Automated systems do not by themselves determine Hunt outcomes.

Whenever an AI system flags a submission that could affect a user's stake, payout, or eligibility, the submission is reviewed by a person before a final decision is made.

Users may dispute verification decisions in accordance with our Terms of Service.

AI Training

We do not use your proof submissions to train publicly available third-party AI models.

Where available, we configure AI service providers to process submissions without using them to improve or train public foundation models.

Marketing

With your consent, we may send newsletters, announcements, promotional emails, and product updates.

You may unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link in our emails or through your account settings.

Certain transactional communications—such as payment confirmations, dispute notices, proof reminders, and security notifications—cannot be disabled while you have an active account or active Hunt.

4Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable, we process personal information based on one or more of the following legal grounds:

  • Performance of our contract with you

  • Compliance with legal obligations

5Your Content and Public Visibility

Your proof submissions—including photos, videos, written reflections, and other verification materials—are private by default.

Proof submissions are visible only to:

  • You
  • Authorized Huntz personnel responsible for verification, moderation, fraud prevention, or customer support
  • Trusted service providers assisting us with verification and fraud detection

Other Hunt participants may see limited profile information associated with your participation, including:

  • Username
  • Profile photo
  • Hunt participation
  • Completion status

Users control whether to generate or publicly share share-cards or similar social content.

Huntz will never use your proof submissions, photos, or videos in our own advertising or marketing without your separate, explicit consent.

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.

These safeguards include, where appropriate:

  • Encryption of sensitive information at rest
  • Role-based access controls limiting employee access to information needed for their job responsibilities
  • Authentication and authorization controls
  • Security monitoring and logging
  • Regular security reviews and vulnerability management
