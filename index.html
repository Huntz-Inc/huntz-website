FAQ

Asked plainly, answered plainly.

The five we get every day. Not here? team@huntz.ai

▶ Is this gambling?

No. There is no chance anywhere in Huntz. Your outcome depends entirely on your own effort: finish and your stake comes back in full. Nothing is random, no one plays against you, and Huntz takes no cut of forfeits.

▶ What happens when I finish?

Your full stake returns, plus a capped share of the stakes forfeited by those who quit. Both are settled to your account within 48 hours of the hunt closing.

▶ What if I miss a day?

Every hunt publishes its rules before you join: allowed misses, grace days, deadlines. Fall short of them and your stake joins the finishers' pool. No surprises, no retroactive changes.

▶ How is proof verified?

Public links, video evidence, written reflection, checked against the hunt's rules: an automated first pass, human review for anything unclear, and disputes handled by Huntz, not the host.

▶ What does Huntz charge?

Only a service fee when you join a hunt. Never a percentage of the pool, never a cent from forfeits. If you finish, the fee is the only thing you've spent.
