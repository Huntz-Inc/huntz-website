THE COMPANY

Goals deserve consequences.

Most goals fail quietly. You skip a day, nothing happens, and you skip another. Huntz exists to change what happens next.

The observation

Nearly every abandoned goal dies the same way. Not in one dramatic collapse, but in silence: a missed workout with no consequence, a skipped chapter no one saw, an application you never sent. When nothing happens, nothing changes.

Commitment works when three things line up. The rules are clear. Progress is visible. Follow-through is shared with other people. Huntz was built to put all three in one place: a marketplace for accountability.

What Huntz is

Huntz is an accountability challenge platform. You join a structured challenge, called a Hunt, and commit a stake. The rules are published before you join. You submit proof of progress on the published schedule. Finish, and you recover your stake in full plus a share of the stakes forfeited by participants who quit or fall short.

That is the entire model. The mechanics are laid out step by step in how Huntz works, and the thinking behind stake-backed commitment lives on our accountability challenges page.

What we believe

  • Rules first. Every Hunt publishes its complete rules before anyone joins: cadence, duration, proof requirements, allowed misses and grace days, stake, service fee, completion threshold. Frozen at publication. Never changed retroactively.
  • Verification disclosed up front. Submissions are evaluated against the published rules. Automated checks make a first pass; anything unclear gets human review. Disputes are decided by Huntz, acting reasonably. We do not promise absolute proof of anything, and we say so plainly.
  • Effort you can show. Proof is scheduled and defined before you start. If it cannot be shown, it cannot be a rule.
  • No element of chance. Huntz never wagers against you. Not a bank, not a bookmaker, not gambling.
  • One way we get paid. Our only compensation is the service fee shown when you join. Huntz never keeps forfeited stakes; every forfeit is distributed to finishers.
